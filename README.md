## DESCRIPTION:

**Generate Links for your GOG Offline Installers in the CLI**

Magog will use your GOG Cookies from Firefox to Generate/Access CDN-Links for your GOG Offline Installers in the BOURNE-Shell.

On first Launch, make sure you're logged in to your GOG Account on Firefox.
It will check all your games, dlc's and patches, and then add them to a list.

a notable caveat to this approach (which i realize) is, that you have to re-index your whole gog-library whenever a new update drops.

## REGARDING PRIVACY:

Your Cookies **NEVER** leave your Device
(they will reside in: '**$HOME/.magog/.gog-cookie.txt**').
Your Cookies are fetched with the help of [ce](https://github.com/conzZah/ce), originally a bash script which i improved so it could run on bourne, **you are free to audit ANYTHING** i bring to the table on my Github, should you not trust me, which is warranted because you don't know me at all.
